What HearIntel Enables

Not a feature grid. A clinical infrastructure.

The value of HearIntel is not in the list of features — it is in what those features make possible at a clinical, organizational, and public health level.

Better Clinical Records

Structured hearing-health records make clinical information easier to capture, retrieve, and manage. Every encounter is documented in a consistent format — accessible across visits, across years, and eventually across facilities with patient consent.

Connected Data

When a patient presents at a different facility, their history should travel with them — not start again from scratch. HearIntel connects records across facilities through consent-gated sharing, so clinical context is never lost.

Research-Ready Information

De-identified clinical data, aggregated across facilities, becomes a research asset. Appropriately governed access to the Hearing Health Registry enables the kind of evidence generation that hearing health in Africa currently lacks.

Population Intelligence

Individual clinical records, accumulated at scale, create the foundation for understanding hearing-health patterns — by region, by demographic, by type of loss. HearIntel turns routine clinical practice into population-level insight.

Better Decisions

Accumulated clinical data becomes evidence. Evidence supports better decisions — for individual patients, for clinical practice, for organizational planning, for research, and for public health policy.

Trust & Compliance

Health data protection by design, not by policy.

HearIntel handles clinical records and registry data. The architecture reflects that responsibility — not as a compliance checkbox, but as a foundational requirement.

These are not marketing claims. They are architectural decisions that shape every access path in the system.

As HearIntel's compliance architecture matures, this section will become a dedicated Trust Center with full documentation of data flows, access governance, and audit mechanisms.

  • 01
    Consent-gated cross-facility access A patient's record is only visible to another facility when the patient has actively consented — in person, or through a verifiable consent act. Subscription tier does not determine access to patient data.
  • 02
    De-identification before Registry entry Clinical records are de-identified before any data reaches the Hearing Health Registry. The de-identification happens in the pipeline — not as a post-hoc filter.
  • 03
    No re-identification path from Registry data There is no user-facing function that links a Registry record back to an identified HPMS patient. That path does not exist in the system by design.
  • 04
    Licence-verified clinical sign-off Diagnosis, management plans, and care-plan authorization require a verified audiology licence. The system enforces this at the application layer, not through convention.
  • 05
    Server-verified session context on every request Facility identity and role are resolved server-side from a verified session — never accepted from the request body. Cross-tenant access is structurally prevented, not policy-dependent.

Credibility

Real evidence. No manufactured proof.

HearIntel is an early-stage product. The evidence presented here is accurate. Where something doesn't yet exist — a large customer base, published outcomes — it is not invented. As real evidence becomes available, it will appear here.

Domain Expertise

Built with Practicing Audiologists

HearIntel's clinical workflow, assessment structure, and terminology reflect direct involvement from qualified audiology practitioners. The product is designed for how audiology is actually practised — not for how it is described in documentation.

Standards & Calibration

Clinical Standards & Regulatory Alignment

Designed in alignment with Medical Laboratory Science Council (MLSCN) audiological guidelines and calibrated to ISO 8253-1 / ANSI S3.6 pure tone and electrophysiological diagnostic standards.

Phased, honest rollout. HearIntel is currently in pilot stage. We are not claiming a large installed base or published clinical outcomes that do not yet exist. This statement will be updated — with specifics — as deployment progresses.

Roadmap

Where HearIntel is going.

HearIntel is being built as infrastructure — not a product that ships once. The roadmap reflects that ambition without pretending that future capabilities already exist.

Phase 1 — Current

Pilot Deployment

Initial deployment and validation with early clinical partners. Establishing the foundational HPMS workflow and building toward the first Registry data contribution.

Phase 2 — Next

Multi-State Expansion

Expansion across additional facilities and states. Registry contributions begin to aggregate at scale. Research access pathways established with appropriate governance.

Phase 3 — Future

National & Regional

Expansion toward broader national and regional hearing-health infrastructure. HearIntel becomes the data layer for population-level hearing health policy and evidence generation.
